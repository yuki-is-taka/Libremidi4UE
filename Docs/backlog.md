| **Keep libremidi's implementation out of Libremidi4UE's public headers (exceptions follow-up 2)** - `Public/LibremidiEngineSubsystem.h` includes `<libremidi/libremidi.hpp>` (for `TUniquePtr<libremidi::observer>` and a few port types), and `libremidi.Build.cs` exports `LIBREMIDI_HEADER_ONLY` publicly, so every module that includes the header compiles all of libremidi's backends, Windows MIDI Services and C++/WinRT included, in its own translation units. ### Outside the UMP-only scope — recorded for later (owner decision 2026-10-03)

The 2026-10 fix batch is scoped to the UMP path over Windows MIDI Services
([adr-0003](decisions/adr-0003-libremidi-fork-strategy.md) rule 8). A consumer that reaches every
device through Windows MIDI Services as UMP (MIDI 1.0 devices through the service's up-conversion,
UMP words sent through libremidi's UMP output, inputs opened with MIDI 1 channel events upgraded
to MIDI 2) does not hit the defects below: they sit on libremidi's MIDI 1 byte-API paths, in the
WinMM backend, or only on virtual ports. They are recorded here on purpose instead of as upstream
GitHub issues. Several are still reachable through Libremidi4UE's own `SendMessage` and `Midi1`
inputs; the rows say where. (q) and (p) were moved into the batch (owner decision D4).
